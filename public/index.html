Two ways to enhance, one workflow

Pick the mode that fits your footage and your network.

⚡

Local Enhance

Runs entirely through ffmpeg on the server — no API key, no external calls. Denoises, upscales 2x with Lanczos, sharpens, lifts contrast & saturation, and interpolates motion up to 60fps.

🧠

AI Enhance

Extracts every frame and runs it through a hosted super-resolution model (Swin2SR) via the HuggingFace Inference API, then reassembles the video for the sharpest possible result.

🔌

Bring your own model

The enhance pipeline is pluggable — point it at another inference provider and swap in your own model without touching the upload or playback flow.

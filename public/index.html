Frequently asked questions

Does Nova train on our data?

No. Your data is used only to answer questions and run workflows inside your own workspace — never to train shared models.

What tools does Nova connect to?

Nova integrates with common docs, chat, and ticketing tools, plus a general-purpose connector for anything with an API.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — plans are month to month with no lock-in, and you can export your workflows and data whenever you like.

Is there a free trial for the Team plan?

Yes, every Team plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required to begin.

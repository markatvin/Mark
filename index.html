The Craft Philosophy

Every piece Mark crafts is choreographed like a kinetic sculpture. Using steaming, bending, and carving techniques, he unlocks shapes that wood rarely offers willingly.

Custom lamination, charred shou sugi ban finishes, and reclaimed hardwoods for a timeless patina.

3D scanning and CNC milling map the vision; hand tools perfect each detail for a human touch.

Forest-friendly sourcing, zero-waste joinery, and natural finishes respect the environment.
